* Download the NX1 files from the link below:
https://archive.org/download/cod-xbogs-freesixty-nx-1/

* Take the downloaded files and transfer them to your Xbox 360 (via USB, FatXplorer, Neighborhood, etc), wherever you store your games 
  i.e. HDD:\Games\NX1[game files]

* Grab the two .xex files from the "Patched" folder and the "raw" folder. Place them into the root of your NX1 directory

* Take the HvP2.xex and NX1Injector.xex and place them on the root of your console. Set HvP2.xex as a plugin in Dashlaunch or load it with a module loader

* Launch NX1. I use the 5-nx1mp_demo(depatch).xex but either of the two should work the same

* It will take a short period of time for the game to load, just be patient. You will sit on a black screen seemingly frozen, then the game will load, and then it will freeze on the mp screen. Just give it a couple minutes and you'll know it's working when you hear the menu music playing

* Click on System Link but dont start the game

* Using your module loader, load NX1Injector.xex and start the game!
